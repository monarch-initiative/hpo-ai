- G6PD is an enzyme. It is clinically measured as enzymatic activity (typically in U/g Hb or U/10¹² RBC), not as a molar or mass concentration. The old labels used the vague "level", which was imprecise but at least not wrong. Replacing it with "concentration" is a step in the wrong direction. Notably, the refactoring correctly used "activity" for the analogous HP:0003333 (beta-hexosaminidase). The G6PD trio should follow the same pattern and use "activity" as well, otherwise it is internally inconsistent and scientifically inaccurate.
